Indian Income Tax Guide - FY 2025-26

NEW TAX REGIME (Default):
- Income up to Rs 4,00,000: NIL
- Rs 4,00,001 to Rs 8,00,000: 5%
- Rs 8,00,001 to Rs 12,00,000: 10%
- Rs 12,00,001 to Rs 16,00,000: 15%
- Rs 16,00,001 to Rs 20,00,000: 20%
- Rs 20,00,001 to Rs 24,00,000: 25%
- Above Rs 24,00,000: 30%

Standard Deduction: 
For FY 2024-25, standard deduction was increased to Rs 75,000 for the new tax regime.

SECTION 80C DEDUCTIONS (Only applicable under Old Regime):
- Maximum deduction: Rs 1,50,000 per year
- Eligible investments: PPF (Public Provident Fund), EPF (Employee Provident Fund), ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme), NSC (National Savings Certificate), Life Insurance Premium, Tuition Fees, Home Loan Principal.
- ELSS: Shortest lock-in of 3 years among 80C options. Offers market-linked returns. Highly recommended for long-term wealth creation while saving tax.

SECTION 80D DEDUCTIONS:
- Medical Insurance premium deduction up to Rs 25,000 for self, spouse, and dependent children.
- Additional Rs 50,000 allowed for senior citizen parents.

MUTUAL FUNDS:
- Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method to invest a fixed amount regularly.
- Large Cap Mutual Funds: Invest in top 100 Indian companies. Low risk, moderate returns.
- Mid Cap Mutual Funds: High risk, higher growth potential.
- Small Cap Mutual Funds: Extremely high risk and high volatility, but highest long-term return potential.

FINANCIAL PLANNING BASICS:
- Always maintain an emergency fund equivalent to 6 months of living expenses in a liquid fund or savings account.
- 50-30-20 Rule: 50% for Needs, 30% for Wants, 20% for Savings.
